Nia Universal Device Bridge

Connect your tablet to another compatible browser on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. WebRTC encrypts the peer-to-peer text channel. The separate Nia Companion app is not connected yet; device controls, cameras, files, and microphones remain inaccessible.
